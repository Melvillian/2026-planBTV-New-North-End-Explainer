# planBTV New North End: draft changes, June 22 → October 20, 2026

**Old:** `2026-06-22_planBTV_New North End_Compressed_…pdf`, 219 pages. This is the draft named in `website-content-sources.md` on `main`, and the one the site is built from.
**New:** `261020_planBTV_NNE_ReducedSize_202610201321106988.pdf`, 209 pages. The cover says "Updated 9/18/2026".

How this was made: I extracted text page by page with `pdftotext`, aligned the two documents' pages by text similarity, and compared them sentence by sentence. Top / middle / bottom means the vertical third of the **new** page where the change sits, measured from word coordinates (`pdftotext -bbox`). I checked the map/diagram changes on pp. 15 and 111 by rendering the pages.

> **Baseline caveat:** `planBTV-sha256.txt` on `main` (`1bfffd20…`) doesn't match any PDF on disk. The ~/Downloads copy of the June 22 draft hashes to `8a1f9c16…`. Its page count (219) and page layout match every citation in `website-content-sources.md`, so I used it as the baseline. An August 20 version also exists in the main checkout (`planBTV.pdf`, 209 pages), along with uncommitted site edits based on it. That version is **not** on `main` and wasn't used here.

---

## Headline change

**The Ethan Allen Parkway and Starr Farm West (Starr Farm Park) housing sites were removed.** The plan now has **three** focus areas, all privately owned: North Ave Alliance Church, Ethan Allen Plaza, and Plattsburg Ave at North Ave. It now says outright that City parks "are not and will not be considered as housing sites." Most of the other changes follow from this.
